## Summary

> Clinical trials and preclinical studies provide strong evidence that TRPC6 inhibition is a viable therapeutic strategy for FSGS. The phase II clinical trial of BI 764198, a TRPC6 inhibitor, in people with FSGS (NCT05213624) has completed, suggesting the target's relevance to the disease. Preclinical studies show that TRPC6 knockout or pharmacological inhibition can reduce kidney disease severity and proteinuria in various models.

---

## Analysis

TRPC6 inhibitors have been tested clinically for FSGS with a phase II trial of BI 764198, which has completed (NCT05213624). Preclinical studies indicate that TRPC6 knockout or pharmacological inhibition can reduce the severity and proteinuria in various models. These findings support the therapeutic potential of targeting TRPC6 for FSGS.
