## Analysis

TRPC6 has been extensively studied both preclinically and clinically for its role in FSGS. Multiple articles highlight TRPC6's involvement in podocyte injury and proteinuric kidney diseases, with several studies demonstrating that TRPC6 mutations can cause FSGS. BI 764198, a TRPC6 inhibitor, is currently being tested in Phase 2 (NCT05213624) and Phase 3 (NCT07220083) clinical trials for FSGS. These trials are sponsored by Boehringer Ingelheim and are ongoing or completed, indicating substantial investment from the pharmaceutical industry. The Phase 2 trial showed proteinuria response in some participants, suggesting therapeutic benefit. However, the lack of definitive primary endpoint results in the Phase 3 trial leaves the overall efficacy uncertain.
[⚠ CLINICAL TRIAL GUARD: NCT05213624 is described as Phase 3, but the registry records it as PHASE2 (Phase 2).]
