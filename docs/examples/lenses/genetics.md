## Analysis

The genetic evidence strongly supports TRPC6 as a causal driver of focal segmental glomerulosclerosis (FSGS). Mendelian-grade variant data from ClinVar confirm the gene's role in FSGS, with over 40 pathogenic/likely-pathogenic variants reported. The absence of GWAS signal is expected for a rare Mendelian disease and must not be cited to lower causality confidence. Constraint evidence shows TRPC6 to be relatively LoF-tolerant (LOEUF=0.759) and missense-tolerant (mis_z=1.70), consistent with gain-of-function mechanisms. The preponderance of pathogenic missense variants in ClinVar, along with the reported Autosomal dominant inheritance, strongly supports a gain-of-function mechanism for TRPC6 in FSGS.
